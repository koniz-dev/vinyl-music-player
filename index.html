Browser Support

    Help & guide

    What do you want to make?

    Start with one recipe, or open a group below to see every tool and its privacy or download requirements.

    Core workflow
    Audio, title & artist

    Drop MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A, or AAC. Title, artist, and embedded cover can be filled from the file’s ID3 metadata.

    Playback controls

    Preview the finished look while listening. Space plays/pauses; left and right arrow keys seek 5 seconds when you are not typing.

    Export

    Set aspect ratio, MP4/WebM when supported, and an optional mm:ss segment. Export stays on your device.

    Offline & browser support

    The app is installable and works offline after its first load. Use the info button beside Settings to check the export formats your browser supports.

    Lyrics
    Manual lines and import

    Add timed lines one by one, or import JSON/LRC. Export LRC later from More lyric tools.

    Auto-sync with AI

    Paste plain lyrics one line per row, choose Tiny (~40 MB) or Small (~250 MB), and run Whisper locally. Leave it blank to transcribe from scratch.

    Find, translate & export lyrics

    Find lyrics sends only track metadata to LRCLIB after confirmation. Translation is local but downloads NLLB (over 1 GB) after confirmation; you can remove it here.

    Artwork & appearance
    Album artwork

    Upload an image for the record label and ambient background. Generate cover sends only your prompt to Pollinations after confirmation.

    Palette, colors & font

    Choose a palette or customize individual colors; the first palette can derive colors from your album art. Pick a font for song text and lyrics.

    Enhancements

    Add an export-only audio-reactive visualizer or ask the app to suggest a palette and font from the first 30 seconds of audio. Both stay local.

    Privacy at a glance: audio, lyrics, preview, AI timing, translation, and export remain in this browser. Only the explicitly confirmed LRCLIB metadata lookup and Pollinations cover prompt leave your device; optional model and font files download from their stated providers.
